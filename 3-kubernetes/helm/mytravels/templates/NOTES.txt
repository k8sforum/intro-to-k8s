MyTravels was installed into namespace "{{ .Values.namespace.name }}".

1. Wait for the db-migrations Job to complete before relying on the API/Messaging services
   (they will crash-loop until the schema is migrated — Kubernetes will keep restarting them):

     kubectl wait job/db-migrations -n {{ .Values.namespace.name }} --for=condition=Complete --timeout=300s
     kubectl logs -n {{ .Values.namespace.name }} -l job-name=db-migrations -c migrate-core-db

   Re-running `helm upgrade`? The Job is immutable — delete it first:
     kubectl delete job db-migrations -n {{ .Values.namespace.name }}

2. Check overall status:

     kubectl get pods,pvc,svc,ingress -n {{ .Values.namespace.name }}
